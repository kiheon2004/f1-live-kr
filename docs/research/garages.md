## KEY SOURCE FAMILY: FIA "Event Notes / Competition Notes – Circuit Map, Pit Lane Drawing ..." (fia.com decision documents)

For almost every 2025 and 2026 F1 event the FIA Race Director publishes a "PIT LANE DRAWING" page showing every garage in the
pit building with its number and the team/organisation assigned, the pit-stop box positions, and where the pit lane starts/ends.
I downloaded all of them (2025: 24 events; 2026: 17 events published so far) from
`https://www.fia.com/system/files/decision-document/<file>.pdf` (listing pages:
https://www.fia.com/documents/championships/fia-formula-one-world-championship-14/season/season-2025-2071 and .../season-2026-2072,
per-event pages .../event/<Event%20Name>) and read the drawing pages myself (rendered to PNG; label-to-number mapping verified with PyMuPDF word coordinates where crowded).
Note: the garage strip in these drawings is schematic (equal-width boxes) — it gives allocation and order, not true bay widths.
Grey/blank boxes in the strip = gaps/unallocated/structural (counted separately).

## Notes / caveats
- FIA drawings show the F1 allocation only. Support-series (F2/F3/Supercup/F1 Academy) garages are not shown; where a building has more bays than the drawing (grey ends: COTA, Mexico, Las Vegas, Sakhir, Yas), the extra count is **unknown**.
- "Split" garage = one numbered garage shared by two adjacent teams (0.5 each). "Half-grey" box (Monza 2026, Montréal 2026, Madring 1) = garage drawn partially greyed; meaning not stated.
- Per-driver work spaces: drawings never label car vs ops/spare bays. Each team runs 2 cars; with 3+ bays the extra is ops/engineering/storage (EST). With 2 bays (Interlagos, Madring) = 1 per car. Monaco = 1 large garage per team (3 sections drawn in 2025).
- Pit-stop boxes: exactly one per team in front of its garages in every drawing.
- Team order follows previous-year constructors' standings from the FIA/FOM end; that end is the pit ENTRY at most circuits but the EXIT end is where the top teams sit at Albert Park and Suzuka (reversed numbering), and Montréal runs back-markers→McLaren from entry to exit.
- 2026 documents not yet published (2026-10-18): COTA, Mexico, Interlagos, Las Vegas, Lusail, Yas Marina. No 2026 event for Imola, Jeddah, Sakhir in the FIA listing (2026 Bahrain GP ran at Sepang).
