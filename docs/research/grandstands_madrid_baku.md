# Part B (fork): Madring (Madrid) and Baku City Circuit

Research date: 2026-10-16. Both 2026 events are over (Madrid 11–13 Sep 2026, Baku 24–26 Sep 2026). The live official ticket pages now show **2027** (Madrid) or **empty/closed** sales (Baku). I therefore took the 2026 data from Wayback Machine copies of the official pages, captured before each event.

---

## Madring (Madrid), 2026 Spanish GP (first edition)

### Source status
- `https://www.madring.com/en/tickets` (live, fetched 2026-10-16): it now says "Official tickets for **2027**". It uses a new 2027 scheme: tiers Gold/Silver/Bronze, Zones A/C/E, and sections 1A, 1B, 1C, 3, 4A, 10, 12B, 12C, 13A, 14A, 14B, 15. **That is 2027 only and must not be used for 2026.** Its map page (`/en/map-facilities`) says "MAP UNDER CONSTRUCTION … 2027".
- 2026 data: Wayback copy `web.archive.org/web/20260607132312/https://www.madring.com/en/tickets`, captured 7 Jun 2026 (final 2026 sales state). An earlier copy, `20260115`, has the same sections with shorter text. A 2025-09-15 copy shows only "tickets go on sale on September 23" and lists no sections.
- Press note `https://www.madring.com/notas-prensa/madring-ya-esta-listo` (pre-race week, Sep 2026, in Spanish) loads. It says tickets sold out and about 350,000 attendees were expected. It says La Monumental is **turn 12**, 547.82 m long, with up to 24 % banking and about 10 m of elevation change. It has nothing on individual grandstands.
- Press note `https://www.madring.com/notas-prensa/programa-official-hospitality` (MATCH Hospitality): about 20,000 hospitality/VIP places. It says hospitality spaces sit "**sobre la curva peraltada de La Monumental**", plus "una tribuna con vistas al podio" and a space next to the paddock. It describes the track as having 22 corners.
- tickets.formula1.com Wayback copy `20260605152046` of `/en/f1-77449-madrid-spain-gp`: lists "Sección 1 (The G01 …)", "Sección 12 (… Peraltada Monumental zone)", "Sección 3 (The G04 grandstand …)", "Sección 7", "Sección 16" and "Sección 10", all sold out. **Note:** this page calls Sección 3 "G04". Its internal G-codes do not match the section numbers, so treat the codes as unreliable.
- Secondary source (stadiaworld.com, 16 Jul 2026): NÜSSLI built the grandstands as **temporary** structures with more than 92,000 seats, in Gold/Silver/Silver Plus/Bronze, some covered and some open, with accessible platforms. Hospitality sits behind the grandstands at turns 2–3.
- The track/turn descriptions come from `https://www.madring.com/en/circuit` (live). T1 comes after a 589 m main straight. T2 is a slow right-hander. T3 "Hortaleza" leads onto the 837 m Ribera del Sena straight. T4 is flat out. T5 is heavy braking. T6–T7 is the "Subida de las Cárcavas" (8 % climb; T7 blind, highest point). T8 is "El Búnker". The Valdebebas section follows. **This live page may already show the 2027 layout** (it shows a "Curve 23"), so its turn numbers may differ from 2026. 2026 material says 22 corners.

All 2026 grandstands are temporary (NÜSSLI). The official 2026 page names stands only as "Section N". It groups them into map areas: Recta Principal, Alta Velocidad, Chicane, Peraltada Monumental, Curvas Enlazadas and Pelouse. Each section names the fan zone it gives access to (Bulevar Cibeles, Bulevar Sol, Barrio, Distrito, Monumental). **Fan-zone names are not grandstand names.** "Section" is a ticket zone. The sources do not say whether each section is a separate structure.

### Differences from the app's list (Madrid)
- The app's names (**"Bulevar Sol B1-B3", "Barrio GC1-GC2", "Barrio GC3-GC5", "Distrito GE18-GE22"**) **appear in none of the official 2026 sources checked**. "Bulevar Sol", "Barrio" and "Distrito" are official **fan-zone** names that sections give access to. The "GC/GE/B" codes were not found anywhere official, and are probably OSM or third-party labels. Rough mapping, by inference: Bulevar Sol → Section 2A; Barrio → Sections 3, 4, 5 (and the 3A GA platform); Distrito → Sections 6, 7, 10–16.
- "La Monumental (outside)" and "La Monumental (inside) (roof)": official 2026 Monumental sections are 9 and 9A (roof not stated), 11 (covered, at the exit) and 10/12. Inside versus outside of the bowl is **not stated** in any official source.
- **Missing from the app:** Sections 1 and 2 (covered Gold main straight, in front of the pits), 6, 7, 8, 8A, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16.
- **Not a grandstand:** Section 3A (GA platform), Section 17 / Pelouse (GA), and the MATCH hospitality areas.
- The 2027 page has a different scheme (Zones A/C/E, sections 1A–15). Do not mix it into 2026 data.

## Baku City Circuit, 2025 and 2026 Azerbaijan GP

### Source status
- `https://www.bakucitycircuit.com/news/official-media-kit` (live): it only embeds a Yumpu document, "Formula 1 2018 Azerbaijan Grand Prix Official Media Kit" (yumpu doc 60053551, dated 24.04.2018). That is **out of date (2018)**, and no grandstand text could be extracted from it. Not used.
- `https://www.bakucitycircuit.com/news/new-grandstand-installed-for-the-10th-anniversary-of-the-formula-1-azerbaijan-grand-prix` (live, published 2026-09-03 according to page metadata) says a new **"City"** grandstand was built for 2026, "**in front of the Azerbaijan State Academic Musical Theatre**". It holds **678** spectators and looks onto "two high-speed sections of the circuit running parallel to each other". Total grandstand capacity is now over 21,000. "Over the past three years, four new grandstands have been installed".
- BCC news of 2025-05-21: "two additional grandstands" for 2025 (+1,500 seats, total over 20,000). BCC news of 2025-09-10: "Tickets for 10 of the 12 grandstands have already sold out". It also says the event has two new grandstands and the first F1 Champions Club.
- The official store `https://www.bakucitycircuit.com/buy-now` now returns an empty list (sales closed). I used the server-rendered data in Wayback captures: **20250324**, **20250920** (2025 event) and **20260826** (2026 event). - I also checked the live API `api.bakucitycircuit.com/en/grandstands`: groups are empty and the products endpoint answers "Online sales are currently closed".

### Differences from the app's list (Baku)
- The app's numeric prefixes (4 Icherisheher, 5 Azneft, 5B Filarmoniya, 5C, 6 Qiz Qalasi, 7 Mugham, 8-9 Sahil/Bulvar, 10 Khazar, 11 Zafar, 12 Champions, 13 Marine) **are not used by the official 2025/2026 store**, which uses names only. Their origin (probably OSM or older material) is unverified.
- **"5C"**: no official grandstand or sector with this name was found in 2025 or 2026. It may be an OSM artefact or an older code. Treat it as unverified. It is not Absheron C, which is a sector of the main grandstand.
- **"8-9 Sahil / Bulvar"**: official sources list Sahil and Bulvar as two separate products. Whether they are one structure is unknown.
- **"Absheron"**: one main grandstand sold as sectors A, B, C Upper, C Lower, D and E. Only sector C is covered, with upper and lower levels. The app should not mark the whole stand as covered. It should not split it into 5–6 stands either, unless real separate structures are confirmed.
- "6 Qiz Qalasi" is officially spelled **Giz Galasi**. "4 Icherisheher" is officially **Icheri Sheher**. "5 Azneft" was renamed **PASHA Real Estate (Azneft)** for 2026 (sponsor rename only).
- **Missing from the app:** **City** (new 2026, 678 seats, in front of the Azerbaijan State Academic Musical Theatre).
- **Hospitality/GA, not grandstands:** Paddock Club, Champions Club, VIPLANE Lounge, General Admission.
