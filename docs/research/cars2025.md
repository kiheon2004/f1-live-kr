# 2025 F1 race cars: visual/technical reference (research only)

Compiled 2026-10-16. Confidence: **H** = stated in a priority source (F1.com Hughes/Piola, The Race/Gary Anderson, FIA text); **M** = secondary tech site (PlanetF1/Somerfield, Crash, autoracer) or several consistent sources; **L** = my own recollection or inference, not confirmed by a fetched source. Treat L items as placeholders to check against photos.

Not recorded: no exact dimensions for any team part (none are published). Mirror mounts, DRS pod, beam wing and matte/gloss finish are poorly covered in text sources, so most of those entries are L.

## 0. Show cars vs raced cars (read first)

- **F1 75 Live, The O2, London, 18 Feb 2025**: all 10 liveries shown at once. Several cars on stage were **show cars or renders with old bodywork** (Sauber had a show car on stage; Racing Bulls' launch renders still had 2024 bodywork, per PlanetF1/The Race). Use these images for **livery only**, not for shapes.
- Real-car sources by team: McLaren Silverstone filming day 13 Feb (camo livery); Williams Silverstone shakedown 14 Feb (camo livery); Haas private Silverstone shakedown, weekend before 18 Feb; Ferrari Fiorano shakedown 19 Feb; Racing Bulls Imola shakedown 19 Feb (Wed); Aston Martin online reveal 23 Feb, track debut Bahrain 24 Feb; Alpine first run Bahrain 24 Feb; Mercedes renders 24 Feb, Bahrain shakedown 25 Feb; Red Bull Bahrain filming day 25 Feb; pre-season test Bahrain 26–28 Feb; first race Australia 16 Mar.
- Red Bull's raced nose (from Bahrain test day 3) is **not** the launch/early-test nose.

## 1. Regulations: key dimensions 2022–2025 (FIA)

Source: *FIA 2025 Formula 1 Technical Regulations*, Issue 03, 07 Apr 2025 (PDF read directly): https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/documents/fia_2025_formula_1_technical_regulations_-_issue_03_-_2025-04-07.pdf (the same values apply across the 2022–2025 rule set).

| Item | Value | Reference | Conf |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overall width | no part outboard of Y=1000 (i.e. **2000 mm** total), except tyres, rims (10.7.2) and wheel covers (3.13.7) | Art. 3.4.1 | H |
| Wheelbase | XF=0 to XR=0 **≤ 3600 mm** | Art. 3.4.2 | H |
| Front wheel position | XF=0 not behind XA=100 | Art. 3.4.3 | H |
| Rim (18 in) | front tyre mounting width 335.3 ±0.5 mm; rear 429.3 ±0.5 mm; outer lip diameter 490.6 ±1 mm (both) | Art. 10.7.2 | H |
| Tyre width (FIA measured, new, 1.4 bar) | front **345–375 mm**, rear **440–470 mm** | Art. 10.8.1(b) | H |
| Tyre diameter | **≤ 725 mm dry**, ≤ 735 mm wet | Art. 10.8.1(c) | H |
| Suspension width | front wheel axis origin ≤ Y=635; rear ≤ Y=548 | Art. 10.10.1 | H |
| Pirelli nominal figures | **tread** width 305 mm front / 405 mm rear; overall diameter **720 mm** (660 → 720 mm in 2022) | Pirelli 2022 launch, via tyrepress.com 2021-07 (https://tyrepress.com/2021/07/a-fresh-chapter-pirelli-presents-18-inch-f1-tyres-at-silverstone) | M |

Note: 305/405 mm is Pirelli's tread width. The FIA 345–375/440–470 mm range is overall tyre width, so the two sets of numbers measure different things. For a model, use about 720 mm diameter (FIA cap 725), 18-inch rims, and overall width of about 360 mm front and 455 mm rear (midpoints of the FIA ranges; these midpoints are my estimate).

## 6. Summary

- **Suspension (H for 8 of 10)**: pull front on Red Bull, Racing Bulls, McLaren, Ferrari (new for 2025) and Sauber; push front on the rest. Ferrari, Haas and Sauber are pull at the rear (Sauber only M, sources conflict). Everyone else is push at the rear, including Williams (new for 2025).
- **Sidepods**: gully/bathtub tops on Ferrari, Aston Martin, Williams and Sauber; downwash ramps on Red Bull, McLaren, Mercedes, Racing Bulls, Haas and Alpine. Inlets: P-shaped on Ferrari, McLaren, Aston and Alpine; letterbox plus vertical on Red Bull and Racing Bulls; Mercedes switched to a wide vertical inlet; hooped/wide on Haas, Williams and Sauber.
- **Noses**: Red Bull is short, with the tip on the 2nd element from end of testing. Mercedes and Haas are long. Ferrari and Aston are wide and boxy with a slot gap. The rest are L and need photo checks.
- **Don't model from**: the O2 show cars or the Racing Bulls launch renders (2024 bodywork), the McLaren/Williams camo shakedown liveries, or Red Bull's early-test nose.
- **Biggest raced changes**: Aston Martin (Imola), Sauber (Barcelona), Ferrari (Austria floor, Spa rear suspension), Mercedes (Imola rear suspension, dropped from Hungary), Red Bull (Imola, Monza, Singapore), McLaren (Canada/Austria corners, Silverstone floor).
- **Weak data**: mirror mounts, DRS pod/pillar, beam wings and matte/gloss finishes are mostly L. Only Red Bull's matte navy is well established.
