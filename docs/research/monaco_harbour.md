# Monaco Port Hercule: quays, GP-week yacht mooring, modelling reference

For LiveTimingKR v0.37: Monaco GP (venue key 22), seasons 2025 and 2026 only.
Research date 2026-10-18. Web research only: no app runs and no code edits.

Legend: **[C]** = confirmed by a cited source. **[M]** = measured by me from OSM geometry or Esri imagery (accurate to about ±5–10 m). **[E]** = estimate or inference.

GP dates: 2025 = 22–25 May (SEPM tender form). 2026 = 4–7 June (SEPM GP form). The SEPM GP berth period for 2026 is 1–8 June (MPL brochure).
The sources below cover only the F1 GP. I excluded material on the Yacht Show, the Historic GP and the E-Prix. The one exception is the 2021 SEPM zone map, used as a layout reference and flagged where used.

## 2. Harbour geometry (from OSM coastline and pier ways) [M]

The OSM coastline runs with land on the left and water on the right. Coordinates are WGS84 (lat, lon).
For scale: 1e-5° lat ≈ 1.11 m and 1e-5° lon ≈ 0.80 m at this latitude.

- **Harbour entrance (open sea):** between the head of the north counter-jetty, Jetée Lucciana, at about (43.73640, 7.43000) and the tip of the dike at about (43.73656, 7.43182). The gap is about 150 m [M]. Lights: green beacon "Monte Carlo" at (43.73639, 7.42993), node 1420666084; red beacon at (43.73642, 7.43193), node 1420666083 [C via OSM/NGA].
- **Inner-basin mouth (old harbour entrance):** between the head of the Capitainerie pier at about (43.7357, 7.4270–7.4275) and the head of the Quai Rainier 1er jetty at about (43.7350, 7.4272–7.4277). It is about 70–80 m wide [M].

### Rafting
I found no source saying yachts raft (moor side by side) inside Port Hercule in GP week. S8 says Faith was "flanked by" 70 m yachts, which describes a side-by-side stern-to row, not rafting.
Model each row as yachts stern-to the quay, side by side, with a **2–4 m gap between hulls** [E].
Treat any rafting as a rare extra and leave it out of the model.

## 4. Fairways and water to keep clear

1. **Outer entrance gap.** Between (43.73640, 7.43000) and (43.73656, 7.43182), about 150 m [M].
   - Leave the outer harbour between the Lucciana jetty and the dike open, except for yachts and cruise ships moored alongside the dike.
   - Keep a **≥50 m channel** inboard of the dike yachts. S4 marks this "Channel" and puts marker buoys at the 8 m-draft line.
2. **Inner-basin mouth.** The 70–80 m gap between the Capitainerie pier head (≈43.7357, 7.4272) and the Quai Rainier 1er head (≈43.7350, 7.4275) [M].
   - The fairway runs from the outer entrance SW to this gap.
   - Approximate centreline [E]: (43.73648, 7.43090) → (43.73560, 7.42900) → (43.73535, 7.42740).
3. **Central basin.** Roughly the polygon (43.7357, 7.4250) – (43.7357, 7.4268) – (43.7343, 7.4268) – (43.7343, 7.4237) – (43.7352, 7.4237) [M, open water in imagery].
   - It stays open as the manoeuvring area. It is the water seen in TV shots between the T and the Capitainerie.
   - Yacht sterns line its edges. Their bows point into this area, so allow about 80 m beyond the quay lines for Z1, Z4, Z5 and Z6.
4. **Lanes between finger pontoons** in the south basin (Z9) and in Darse Sud/Nord (Z7/Z8). Keep about a 20–30 m lane between rows of bows [E].

## 5. Water level and heights

- **Datum [C S4].** The dike drawing gives the "Niveau de l'eau EL 0.0000 m" and puts the dike quay top **2.9 m above the water**. Fender centre is at +2.3 m.
- **Tide.** The Mediterranean tide at Monaco is about 0.2–0.3 m, so treat the water as a constant 0 m MSL [E, general knowledge; no source fetched].
- **Inner quays** (Quai des États-Unis, Quai Antoine 1er, Quai Albert 1er, the T): **about 1.0–1.5 m above the water** [E, not sourced]. Use 1.2 m.
- **Floating pontoons** (OSM `floating=yes`: 31900562, 94399635, 1202361395–97): deck about 0.4–0.6 m above the water [E].
- **Depths [C S1].** Basin 2.5 m to 55 m; outer port 40 m; outer jetty 30 m. These do not affect the model.

### Instancing suggestion for GP week [E]
- **Z1 (Quai des États-Unis), 215 m:** about 14–16 yachts at 40–75 m, using slots about 13 m wide.
- **Z4 (the T), 220 m:** about 12–14 yachts at 55–80 m, using slots about 15 m wide.
- **Z11 (dike):** 2–3 yachts at 90–150 m alongside, or one cruise ship of about 250–300 m. 2026 had two cruise ships "at the harbour entrance" [C S8]; the second is likely on the outer side or at anchor [E].
- **Z9/Z7/Z8:** dense small boats of 10–25 m.
- **Z10:** one 80–90 m yacht plus 40–60 m yachts.
- Keep the §4 fairways empty.
