# Wheels and car shapes: update for v0.36 (research only)

Researched 2026-10-17. This builds on `docs/research/wheels.md`, `cars2025.md` and `cars2026.md` in the f1-live-kr repo. Only text was read: no photo was opened or downloaded, so a shape described below always comes from a writer's description or an image caption, never from my own look at a picture.

**Source types:** race photo (captioned at an event) · tech analysis (a journalist's analysis of race-weekend or test photos) · render · show car · replica (licensed collector piece or sim-racing product) · sim (sim-racing wheel, not a replica of a race part) · forum · interview.
**Confidence:** H = primary source or tech analysis of race or test photos, stated outright. M = one credible outlet, or an older season's description that a later source implies still holds. L = forum, sim product, or carry-over from an older season with nothing confirming it.

**Bottom line for wheels.** For Red Bull, Racing Bulls, Aston Martin, Audi, Williams 2026 and Sauber 2025, no 2025 or 2026 text source describes the wheel's outline, ratio, grips, LEDs or button colours. **Not found** is the honest result. What exists is (a) one correction (McLaren 2026), (b) one shape fact for Williams (from 2024, carried into 2025), and (c) 2023 descriptions of how each team's wheel looked, which say a wheel's look changes little from year to year. Those are given as lineage (M/L), not as facts about the season.

## 2. Car shapes: claims resting on renders, show cars or pre-track images

Format for each claim: **claim (doc / code)** → evidence → **verdict**. "Test" means pre-season running on track (not a race), which is better than a render but is not a race weekend. Nothing below came from looking at a photo.

### 2026

## 3. What can change in the code

**Wheels** (`carshapes.js` `wheel`, `car3d.js` wheel body, `wheelscreen.js`)
1. **McLaren 2026 (`mcl40`):** if a second display is drawn, draw it as a **second bar graphic above the gear readout on the same 4.3" screen**: centre to right = deploy, centre to left = harvest, hidden when the MGU-K is idle. Do not add a second physical screen. Source: Motorsport.com 10798120, 2026-02-16, Bahrain test 1 photos (H for the bar, M for one screen). Leave outline, LEDs and button colours unset (pink/yellow is forum-only, L).
2. **Williams 2025 (`fw47`):** a lower half that **tapers to a triangle (V-bottom) rather than a squared-off bottom** is sourced for the FW46 (Motorsport.com 2024-04-03, H). The FW47 is an update of it (M). It would need a new `style` (e.g. `"vbottom"`) in car3d.js; no ratio, LED count or grip colour is sourced. Williams 2026: keep neutral (not found). **Drop the F1 Authentics "FW48" replica as a source** (that page is the 2024 FW46).
3. **Red Bull 2025/2026, Racing Bulls 2025/2026:** no season source. The only description is from 2023 (PlanetF1, L for these seasons): a notched "Space Invader" outline, few multicoloured buttons down each side, a centre column of rotaries, and Racing Bulls' wheel identical to Red Bull's. Recommendation: keep the neutral wheel. At most, give Racing Bulls the same wheel entry as Red Bull (inference). No ratio, LED or grip value can be sourced.
4. **Mercedes 2025 (`w16`):** optional rounded-rectangle with a slight top bend and Russell's rounder lower grip (The Race 2025-07-15, already in wheels.md). Per-driver rotary colours: Russell blue, Antonelli green/white/red (M). Mercedes 2026: Antonelli's wheel changed from the Canadian GP (clutch lever, Automoto.it 2026-05-21, M), but **no visual change is sourced**, so nothing to draw.
