# 2026 F1 race cars: external shape features (for 3D viewer)

Compiled 2026-10-16. This is research only. Confidence: **H** means a primary or official source, or several tech outlets agree. **M** means one credible tech outlet. **L** means a render, a forum post, a snippet only, or sources that conflict. "unknown" means no source was found. Nothing here is invented.

**Caveats**
- f1technical.net (403) and racer.com (429) could not be fetched, so items from them come from search snippets only.
- Many WebFetch results are model summaries of the articles.
- Launch renders and showcars for Red Bull, Alpine, Aston Martin, Ferrari and Haas were not the real cars. Shape data below is from the race or test cars unless marked "render".

## 0. 2026 regulation key dimensions (FIA)

**Source:** FIA 2026 F1 Regulations, Section C (Technical), **Issue 17, 28 Apr 2026**:
https://api.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_c_technical_-_iss_17_-_2026-04-28.pdf
Values were read directly from the PDF. Coordinates are in mm: Y = 0 is the car centreline, XF is relative to the front axle, XR to the rear axle, and Z is height above the reference plane.

| Item | Value | Ref |
|---|---|---|
| Overall width | No part more than 950 mm from Y = 0, so **1900 mm max** (tyres, rims and rim-attached parts excepted) | C2.3.1 |
| Wheelbase | **≤ 3400 mm** (XF = 0 to XR = 0) | C2.3.3 |
| Front wheel position | XF = 0 between XA = 0 and XA = 150 | C2.3.4 |
| Cockpit position | XA to XC: 1830–2030 mm | C2.3.5 |
| Wheel rims | 18 in. Rim diameter 462.5/463, lip external diameter 496 ±0.5 (front and rear). Tyre mounting width front 315 ±0.5, rear 401.3 ±0.5. Overall rim width front 334, rear 420.3 (ref) | C10.7.2 |
| Tyres (Pirelli 2026) | Front about **280 mm wide, 705 mm diameter**; rear about **375 mm wide, 710 mm diameter** (2025: 720 mm diameter). Front 25 mm narrower, rear 30 mm narrower | Not in the FIA text (C10.8 defers to the tyre supplier). Figures from Mercedes-AMG race previews (e.g. https://www.mercedesamgf1.com/news/what-are-the-tyre-options-for-the-2026-british-gp) and F1Technical https://www.f1technical.net/news/28128 |
| Minimum mass | 724 kg + Nominal Tyre Mass (726 kg + tyres in qualifying and sprint qualifying). Commonly quoted as 768 kg with tyres | C4.1 |
| Front wing profiles | Up to **3 elements** ("up to three sections in any Y-plane"). Volume RV-FW-PROFILES spans Y 0–675, so the profiles are about **1350 mm** wide. Z is 60–300 and X runs from XF −1250 to about −475 | C3.10.1, App. §22 |
| Front wing active aero | "FW Primary Flap" (all elements except the forwardmost) and "FW Secondary Flap" (rearmost element only) rotate to reduce incidence in straight-line mode | C3.10.10 |
| Front wing endplate volume | Y 575–680, Z 75–375, XF −1250 to −300, minus a 925 mm diameter wheel-clearance cylinder | App. §23 |
| Nose | Must lie in RV-NOSE, the part of RV-BODY-FRONT ahead of XA = 0. That volume reaches **XF −1300** (1300 mm ahead of the front axle). Nose-tip half-width ≤ 150 mm at the front (Y 0–150) and Z 125–645 at XF −1300. Tangent continuous, no concave curvature in X-planes. Plan-view radii ≥ 20 mm forward of XA and ≥ 45 mm at XA | C3.7.1, App. §12 |
| Rear wing | Up to **3 elements**, with an 8–12 mm slot gap. RV-RW-PROFILES spans Y 0–575, so about **1150 mm** wide, at Z 725–880, XR +165 to +630. Gurney up to 10 mm. Active "Rear Wing Adjuster System" | C3.11.1, C3.11.6, App. §30 |
| Rear wing endplate | Y 345–575 (lower part inboard at Y 345–375 and Z 250–425, upper part at Y 535–575 and up to Z 880) | App. §31 |
| Beam wing | **No beam-wing article exists in Issue 17.** C3.11 covers only Profiles, Endplate, Brace, Pylon, Assembly, Adjuster, Auxiliary and Group. Hughes (F1.com, 9 Feb 2026) says it "is banned for 2026" | C3.11. Some FIA upgrade-form summaries (Aston Martin Hungary, Haas Spa, Ferrari Zandvoort) still say "beam wing"; this probably refers to the Rear Wing Brace or the lower rear-wing assembly (L) |
| Mirrors | **Mirror Inner Stay must join Mirror Body to the Mid Chassis.** An optional Mirror Rear Stay (< 50 mm × 10 mm section) joins it to the Sidepod. The mirror body houses the lateral safety light. So every car has **chassis-side mirrors**, optionally braced to the sidepod; none sit on the halo | C3.7.5 |

**Viewer implication:** draw no beam wing. Mirrors go beside the cockpit on chassis stays. The front wing has 3 elements and the rear wing 2–3.

## Summary

- **Common to all cars (FIA Section C Iss 17, 28 Apr 2026):**
  - 1900 mm max width, 3400 mm max wheelbase, 18 in rims.
  - Tyres about 280×705 mm front and 375×710 mm rear (Pirelli).
  - 3-element front wing about 1350 mm wide; rear wing up to 3 elements about 1150 mm wide; both active.
  - **No beam wing.**
  - **Mirrors must be stayed to the chassis**, optionally braced to the sidepod; none on the halo.
- **Suspension:** push-rod front and rear on most cars.
  - **Pull-rod front:** Alpine, Cadillac and Williams (H).
  - Rear push-rod is likely everywhere. Alpine, Cadillac and Haas rears are conflicting.
- **Strongest visual identifiers for the viewer:**
  - Racing Bulls: huge boxy airbox, smaller from Spa/Hungary.
  - Audi: narrow vertical inlet hugging the chassis.
  - Aston Martin: wide pelican nose, tiny letterbox inlets, tube pods with a big exposed floor, matte green.
  - Ferrari: saw-tooth shark fin, triangular airbox, gloss red with white cockpit/engine-cover areas, rotating "Macarena" flap.
  - Mercedes: nose mounted on the 2nd front-wing element.
  - Cadillac and Haas: upper outer endplate blade.
  - McLaren: Baku "Audi-like" square inlet and H-wing.
  - Red Bull: shrink-wrapped pods, full downwash from Japan, gloss blue.
  - Williams: gloss blue with a black side band and red/white keyline.
- **Launch versus race:**
  - Red Bull, Alpine and Aston Martin launches used showcars; Ferrari and Haas used renders. Do not model from those.
  - Major in-season changes: RB22 Japan/Austria/Baku; AMR26 Hungary B-spec; Audi Bahrain spec 2 and Baku; Cadillac Austria (shape and livery); McLaren Miami/Baku; Mercedes Sepang; Racing Bulls Spa/Hungary; Alpine Zandvoort; Williams Baku.
- **Gaps:**
  - Nose length and tip height for most teams.
  - Halo fairings.
  - Airbox shapes for Alpine, Aston Martin, Haas, Mercedes, Red Bull and Williams.
  - Finish (matte/gloss) for Mercedes, Haas, Audi, Cadillac, McLaren and Racing Bulls.
  - Exact livery region boundaries.
  - The best next sources are the per-event FIA "car presentation submissions" PDFs (fia.com decision documents) and the F1.com Tech Weekly galleries.
