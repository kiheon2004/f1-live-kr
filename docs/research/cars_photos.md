# 2025 / 2026 race-car photos compared for the 3D cars (v0.39)

How they were used: each team car was rendered from fixed cameras (side, ¾ front, ¾ rear, front, plan, T-cam) and set
against these photos. For the side views, the render was scaled, turned and shifted so that its two wheel hubs landed on
the photo's hubs, then blended 50/50 with the photo (tool: v039 run/tt_cars compare.py). The photos are not shipped
with the app, and no logos were copied: only the colour zones and the shapes were taken from them.

Measured on the hub-aligned side photos (the helmet's middle behind the front axle, from wheelbase pixels; ±0.05 m,
because the cars aren't quite side-on):
- 2025: SF-25 (Japanese GP FP1, 2025-04-04) 1.47 m; MCL39 (Japanese GP FP1) 1.62 m; W16 (Italian GP 2025) 1.57 m.
  The model had 1.88 m. v0.39 moves the cockpit 0.33 m forward (helmet 1.55 m).
- 2026: SF-26 and W17 (Austrian GP FP1, 2026-06-26) 1.45 m and 1.48 m; MCL40 about 1.4 m. The model had 1.75 m.
  v0.39 moves it 0.30 m forward (helmet 1.45 m).
- The T-cam pod: 1.77 m (SF-26) and 1.96 m (MCL39) behind the front axle. v0.39 has it at 1.86 m (2026) and
  1.99 m (2022 profile). The lens was refitted to the v0.22 footage marks (run/tt_cars/tcamfit.py).

## Visual landmarks (from the photos, brief)

### 2025
- **Ferrari SF-25**: The nose is long and slim. Its tip reaches the 1st/2nd front-wing element near the leading edge, and the nose tip is red. The front wing has black lower elements with red endplates and outer flaps. Livery: red overall. A large white block on the engine cover, behind the airbox, carries the blue "HP" logo and runs down to the start of the sidepod; a thin white line separates it from the red. The floor edge and lower sidepod undercut are black/carbon. The rear wing endplates are black, and the upper rear-wing flap is white with HP logos (Las Vegas photo). The wheel covers are red and the halo is red. The sidepod has a low, wide inlet with a deep downwash ramp, and the Shell logo sits on the sidepod flank.
- **McLaren MCL39**: Livery: papaya orange nose, front-wing endplates/top flaps and cockpit surround. The engine cover, sidepod top and rear flank are black (anthracite), with an orange diagonal "swoosh" that rises from the lower sidepod rear to the engine cover and fin. The rear wing endplates and upper flap are orange; the 2025 race photos show the OKX upper flap. The wheel covers are rainbow ("Chrome") at Japan, Austria and Las Vegas. The nose is short-ish, with the tip ending at the 2nd element. The sidepod inlet is high and narrow, over a deep undercut.
- **Red Bull RB21**: Livery: matte dark navy overall. The nose top is a yellow/red bull graphic, and the yellow airbox merges into the red bull on the engine cover. "ORACLE" is white on the sidepod and "Red Bull" red behind it. The rear wing endplates are navy with Mobil 1. The nose is short and wide, ending at the 1st element, with a slotted tip. The sidepod inlet is very low and forward ("letterbox"), with a strong downwash ramp. NOTE: 2025_redbull_side_japan-special-livery.jpg is the one-off white Japan GP livery, not the standard car.
- **Mercedes W16**: Livery: black body. A silver band runs along the nose and chassis top, and a teal (Petronas green) stripe runs from the nose tip along the chassis side to the sidepod. A silver-to-black star pattern covers the engine cover and fin, with "PETRONAS" white on the sidepod. The rear wing upper flap is silver/teal edged. The nose is long and narrow, with its tip at the 2nd element. The sidepod has a high, narrow inlet and a strongly sculpted undercut (row of small floor-edge "teeth" visible in side shot).
- **Aston Martin AMR25**: BRG (dark racing green) overall, with lime-green accents (wing endplates, wheel rims) and "aramco" white on the sidepod.
- **Williams FW47**: Mid/royal blue sidepods and engine cover with a dark-navy nose/lower body. "ATLASSIAN" and "KOMATSU" are white on the sidepod.
- **Haas VF-25**: White upper body with black lower body/floor and red accents on the wing endplates. "HAAS" is black on the white sidepod; the MoneyGram logo is red on the engine cover. (The pink Japan livery was ignored.)
- **Sauber C45**: Black body with fluorescent green nose/front wing and sidepod top, and "Stake" white.
- **Alpine A525**: Blue engine cover/top with pink (BWT) nose, sidepod and wing endplates, and black lower body.
- **Racing Bulls VCARB 02**: White body with a navy/blue lower body and a red/yellow bull on the engine cover. "VISA" is blue on the sidepod.

### 2026 (2026-regulation cars: visibly narrower and shorter than 2025, with simpler front-wing endplates and no wheel-rim covers on most cars except McLaren)
- **Ferrari SF-26**: Livery: red nose and chassis. A large white block covers the cockpit surround/airbox side and engine cover top; it carries the HP logo and its rear edge comes down diagonally to the sidepod top with a red border line. A stepped white "gill" pattern shows on the sidepod top louvres. The rear wing endplates are black. The front wing elements are black with red outer endplates and a small Italian-flag stripe. The wheels are black (no covers). The nose is long and slim and extends to the front-wing leading edge. Blue rear-wing upper flap in Australia (IBM blue).
- **McLaren MCL40**: Livery: orange nose, chassis and front of sidepod. A big black diagonal band runs from the cockpit/airbox down to the rear of the sidepod. The orange rear section runs from behind the band to the rear-wing endplates, which are orange (Dropbox). The airbox side is teal ("allwyn"). The wheel covers are rainbow. The nose is long and thin, ending at the front-wing leading edge.
- **Red Bull RB22**: Livery: glossier navy than RB21, with the yellow airbox flowing into the red bull on the engine cover. "ORACLE" is white and "Red Bull" red on the sidepod, with a Ford oval logo on the rear engine cover. The nose tip is yellow with a red bull, and the front-wing endplates are navy with Mobil. The sidepod inlet is small and high.
- **Mercedes W17**: Livery: black, with a teal stripe and silver along the nose and chassis side, and silver "fang"/stripe graphics over the sidepod louvres. A star pattern covers the engine cover. "Microsoft" sits on the airbox side and "PETRONAS" white on the sidepod. NOTE: the Silverstone 2026 photos on Commons show a mostly silver W17 variant, which was not used.
- **Aston Martin AMR26**: BRG with lime accents, similar zoning to AMR25, and "aramco" white on the sidepod.
- **Cadillac MAC-26**: Silver/white upper body with a black lower body/sidepod undercut, and a large black "TWG" graphic on the white sidepod. Black rear wing with "IFS".
- **Audi R26**: Titanium-silver/grey body with a bright red (Audi "lava red") panel on the rear sidepod/engine cover flank, with "Revolut" on the sidepod. Black rear wing.
- **Williams FW48**: Light/sky blue with darker navy accents on the nose and lower body; "ATLASSIAN" and "KOMATSU" white.
- **Alpine A526**: Pink and blue split: the nose and lower sidepod are pink, with a blue upper body/engine cover and "BWT" on the sidepod.
- **Haas VF-26**: White body with a red lower nose and accents, and a black "GR" (Toyota Gazoo Racing) logo on the engine cover. "HAAS" is black on the sidepod.
- **Racing Bulls VCARB 03**: White body with a navy lower body, a red/yellow bull on the engine cover and "VISA" blue on the sidepod.
