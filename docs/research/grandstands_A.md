# F1 grandstands, group A (2025 / 2026 events)

Research date: 2026-10-16. Research only; no code was changed.

## Conventions
- **Sides** ("left/right") are given in race direction. "INF" marks something I inferred, usually by reading an official schematic map. Everything else is quoted or closely paraphrased from the source.
- **Confidence** is given as **e/l/f/s**: existence / location / facing / shape-size, each H (high), M (medium) or L (low).
- **Map distances:** no pixel distances on unscaled maps were converted to metres.
- **Wayback links:** "WB <timestamp>" means a web.archive.org capture, `https://web.archive.org/web/<timestamp>/<url>`.
- **Working files:** raw downloads are in `scratchpad/research/dl*`, and helper scripts are in `scratchpad/research/scripts*`.

  - Secondary, unconfirmed: grandprix.com (9 Sep 2026) reports a plan for Imola to replace Abu Dhabi on 6 Dec 2026. Nothing official has been announced.
