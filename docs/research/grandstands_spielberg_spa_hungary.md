# Group B (part) — Red Bull Ring, Spa-Francorchamps, Hungaroring

Research date: 2026-10-16. All three official ticket sites now show the **2027** event or a stale 2026 page. 2025/2026 data comes from Wayback snapshots of the official ticket shops and from official maps.
Map reading rule: positions such as "left/right" and "outside/inside" are read topologically from official schematic maps. No distances were derived from them.

Abbreviations used below: "src" is a source, "inf" is an inference, "conf" is confidence, given as exist/loc/facing/shape.

**Differences from the app's list**
- Roof flags are wrong. The app has only Gold 2 and Gold 3 covered. Officially covered: **Gold 1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11; Silver 4**. From 2026 also **Silver 3, Combes 3, Fan Area**, and Combes 2 is partial.
- "Combes 1-2" and "Combes 3-4" merge four ticketed stands. The map draws four separate blocks (C1–C4), and their roof status differs in 2026. Either split them, or keep two merged groups but give them mixed roof status.
