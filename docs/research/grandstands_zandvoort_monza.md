# Part B (fork): Zandvoort and Monza grandstands

Researched 2026-10-16. Both official sites have moved on. dutchgp.com's per-stand pages are now empty shells; the Dutch GP 2026 was billed "The Final Lap / our very last round in Zandvoort". monzanet.it now sells the **2027** event, with its 2027 price list online. 2025 and 2026 data below comes from the official PDFs. I fetched those PDFs directly from the promoter servers. I found their URLs through web.archive.org. Positions are read from official **schematic** maps: no distances are derived from them.

Conventions: "race direction" sides are only given where the official map's flow is unambiguous. Zandvoort runs clockwise (main straight north to Tarzan, a right-hander). For Monza, monzanet.it states "Direction of travel: clockwise", and the official grandstand map has a start arrow pointing toward the Prima Variante. "Inference" marks anything not stated in text.

### Differences from the app's list (Zandvoort)
- All app names exist officially: Tarzan-In, Pit, Main (covered), Ben Pon, Arena-In, Arena-Out, Eastside 1–4 and Hairpin 1–2.
- **"Arena 1–4"**: the official product is a single "Arena Grandstands" (no. 31). The map shows about four separate blocks under that one number, but no official "Arena 1/2/3/4" sub-names were found. Keep them as blocks of one product.
- "Eastside 4" is a separate map number (35) from Eastside 1–3 (33), and it sits on the other leg (T12–T13).
- Missing from the app: nothing seated. GA1, GA2, the decks and the lounges are not grandstands and should not be added as seated stands.
- No covered status is stated for any stand except Main.
- Perm/temp is not stated anywhere official.
