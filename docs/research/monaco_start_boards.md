FIA published **no dimensioned F1 pit-lane drawing** for either year. There are no fast-lane or working-lane widths and no total length on the F1 sheets. The F1 "Pit Lane Drawing" is a schematic with photos. **[C]** for 2025 doc 18 and 2026 doc 7.

### A.1 Pit entry, pit lane path, pit exit

- **Pit entry.** The pit-entry road leaves the track between **Turn 17 (Rascasse)** and **Turn 18 (Anthony Noghès)**, on the inside. The "Pit Entry Status Panels" are FIA light panels 17 and 18. A "60" speed sign is shown in the entry road. **[C]** from the 2025 and 2026 pit lane drawings.
- **Safety Car Line 1.** This is in the pit-entry road. The 2026 photo shows it under an overhead yellow Pirelli banner, with Pirelli boards on both walls. **[C]** from the drawing photo.
- **"Pit Lane Starts".** In 2025 this is the speed-limit start line, just after the entry. **[C]** from the 2025 drawing. 2026 note: "The pit entry barriers had been repositioned since 2025" (The Race, 12 Jun 2026, https://the-race.com/formula-1/the-missing-centimetres-that-forced-f1s-stunning-gasly-penalty-u-turn/). **[C]**
- **Pit lane path.** The lane runs on the **inside of the start/finish straight**. The **garage block sits between the start/finish straight and the pit lane**. The FIA circuit map draws the lane as a thin line between the main straight and the Piscine section (T13–T16).
  - **[C]** Williams, 23 May 2023: the garages "sit between the pit straight and pit lane". The gantry "overlooks the pit box and the swimming pool section exit". https://www.williamsf1.com/posts/e7337645-01a7-4416-bf23-28005c89f6d6/five-things-to-know-ahead-of-the-monaco-grand-prix
  - **[I]** Street names: the main straight is Boulevard Albert 1er, and the pit lane runs on the harbour/swimming-pool side. No FIA document names the streets.
- **The lane is curved.** "Monaco's pitlane, including the entry exit, is curved" (The Race, 12 Jun 2026). **[C]** Both FIA drawings show a gentle curve and a dog-leg near the exit end. **[I]** from the drawings.
- **"Pit Lane Ends" / speed-limit end.** This is at the exit end of the garages. The 2025 drawing places it next to "Pole RHS", meaning pole position is on the right-hand side of the grid, level with the pit-lane end. **[C]** labels from the 2025 drawing. In 2026 the end-of-limit sign (circle with a slash) is drawn near the control line (chequered mark). **[C]** from the drawing.
- **Pit exit road.** After the pit lane ends, a separate pit-exit road runs alongside the track (orange in the 2025 drawing) and rejoins **after Turn 1 (Sainte Dévote)**. The 2025 notes (item 11.4) and 2026 notes (item 13.6) say: "Drivers doing a practice start must use the pit exit road and rejoin the track after turn1 without crossing the white line separating the pit exit road from the track at any time. Drivers not doing a practice start must cross the pit exit white line separating the pit exit road from the track on the LHS, at the earliest opportunity after having left the pit lane and use the normal circuit through turn 1." **[C]**
  - This means the pit exit road lies to the right of the track on the run to T1, separated by a white line, and is crossable during the reconnaissance laps (2026 item 17.2 / 2025 item 14.2). **[C]**
- **Safety Car Line 2.** This is after Turn 1, on the climb toward Beau Rivage. The photo shows the road rising between walls. **[C]** label, **[I]** for the uphill reading of the photo.
- **"Pit Exit Warning Arrows".** These are yellow arrows painted on the track near the pit-exit end (2026 drawing). **[C]**
- **Marshalling boundaries (2026 item 11).** A blue line at the pit entry marks where the sector's flag state stops applying. A blue line at the pit exit marks where the next sector's state starts to apply. Both are shown on drawings. **[C]** The images show the entry line near Rascasse and the exit blue line in the exit road near T1. **[I]** for the exact placement.
- **Length.** **[U]** No official metre figure was found.
  - The 2026 pit lane was lengthened to fit 22 cars (Cadillac), and the fast lane gained a slight curve. ESPN Deportes, 8 Jun 2026: "tuvieron que alargar el carril". https://espndeportes.espn.com/deporte-motor/f1/nota/_/id/16811079/por-que-penalizaciones-pits-gp-monaco-f1-2026 **[C]** (secondary source)
  - The first pit-lane timing zone after the entry is 26.92 m as used by timing (shortest line 26.15 m). The 26.92 m figure is **[C]** (The Race, 12 Jun 2026). A search summary also said there are 9 timing-loop zones of about 10–40 m each, but I could not open the primary source. Treat that as **[I]/unverified**.
  - Pit-stop time loss: 19.4 s including a 2.5 s stop (F1.com 2025 preview, https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/need-to-know-the-most-important-facts-stats-and-trivia-ahead-of-the-2025-monaco-grand-prix.7FbX7r8dNJZQF6jnyLnoJC). **[C]**
  - Suggestion: measure the length from the project's own track geometry or OSM.
- **Speed limit: 60 km/h, confirmed for both years.**
  - 2025 notes: "The Pit Lane Speed limit detailed in Article 34.7 of the Sporting Regulations is hereby amended to 60km/h for the duration of the event." **[C]**
  - 2026 notes item 16: "The Pit Lane Speed limit detailed in Article B1.6.3a of the FIA F1 Regulations is hereby amended to 60km/h for the duration of the event." **[C]**
  - The default limit is 80 km/h (F1.com explainer, 8 Jun 2026). **[C]** https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/explained-why-are-there-pit-lane-speed-limits-in-f1-and-how-are-they-measured.jc4jiWVsfbRhb62QomSf0.jc4jiWVsfbRhb62QomSf0
  - In 2026, seven pit-lane speeding penalties were given at Monaco (ESPN Deportes). The FIA list shows speeding infringements for cars 10, 12, 14, 23, 43, 44, 63 and 81. **[C]**
- **Narrow lane.** Both years' notes say: "This event is at a track where the pit lane is narrow." A released car must blend into the fast lane at once, and it is "in the fast lane when all parts of the car have crossed the line separating the fast lane from the inner lane". **[C]**
- **Fast lane / inner lane line.** A solid white line separates the fast lane from the inner (working) lane. The 2026 notes include a photo of it near the pit exit, with garages on the left and an aramco-branded wall on the right. **[C]**
- **Lane widths.** No F1 figure was found. **[U]**
  - The only dimensioned sheet is the **F2** pit-stop sheet (2026 F2 drawings p.4, "V1 – 03/06/2026"). It shows fast lane → F2 pit-stop zone → F2 equipment → F1 equipment → F1 garage. Distances from the fast-lane edge: 2.7 m to the car centreline, 5.7 m to the end of the F2 working area, and 6.8 m to the F1 team-equipment zone. **[C]** for F2 support-race use.
  - **[I]**: the inner/working lane plus equipment strip in front of an F1 garage is therefore roughly 6.8 m deep, plus the F1 equipment strip. The fast-lane width is not given.
- **Elevation / crossings.** Nothing in the FIA documents shows the pit lane crossing under or over anything. **[U]** for elevation. **[I]**: it is a flat harbour-level lane. The tunnel is far away (T12 area).

**Building, floors and pit-wall stands**

  - "The ground floor is exclusively for things that have to be down there". "On the first floor, you'll find the smaller spares bins and technological equipment… In Monaco, this level is also shared with the pit wall gantry." "The third floor is reserved exclusively for VIPs and hospitality guests." **[C]**
  - Williams numbers the floors as ground, first and "third". The second floor is not described. Treat as **at least 3 levels above ground: [I]**.

Pit-wall personnel work from the upper-floor gantry. **[C]** from Williams and Autosport. **[I]** for the "back of the garage block" geometry, read from the drawings.

- **Materials and colours.**
  - **[I]** from the 2025 FIA "Collection Point" photo and the 2026 "HV Personnel Collection Point" photo: a modern modular building in white/grey with a cantilevered, fully glazed upper floor. The 2026 photo has Louis Vuitton branding at its base.
  - Pit-lane surface: dark red/maroon at the pit exit (2025 "Pit Lane Ends" photo and 2026 "Pit Exit" photo), with white lines. TAG Heuer barrier boards appeared in 2025.
  - **[C]** that the photos exist; **[I]** for the descriptions.
- **Notes on garage use (2025 and 2026).** Support-race team barriers must be no more than 1 m from the garage. Pit-stop gantry arms must be moved back during support sessions. **[C]**

### A.3 Pit exit light, pit-lane start queue, other points

- **Pit exit light.**
  - 2025 photo ("Pit Lane Ends"): a red/green light cluster stands on the **left-hand side** at the end of the pit lane, on the wall between the exit road and the track. A white end-of-speed-limit sign (circle with a slash) is on the **right-hand** wall. **[C]** that this is the photo; **[I]** for the left/right reading.
  - 2026 "Pit Exit" photo shows the same arrangement, with the red light illuminated on the left. **[I]**
- **Pit-lane starters' queue.** Cars "must stop in a line in the Fast Lane" at the pit exit (B5.3.1 / B5.7.3; 2025 Art. 44.11). **[C]**
  - **No practice starts at pit exit** (2026 item 13.1; 2025 item 11.1). During the reconnaissance laps only, practice starts "may be done in the pit exit road in the area of the 100 meter brake marker board on the RHS". **[C]**
  - In 2026 Pérez (car 11) was reprimanded for a practice start in the wrong position (doc 86, 07 Jun 2026). **[C]**
- **Red flag / suspension.** "cars will be stopped in the fast lane. The first car must stop in the vicinity of the last garage." (2026 item 25.1; 2025 item 28 is similar.) **[C]**
  - For the resumption, "Safety Car will leave the pit lane 1 minute before the resumption and wait for the F1 cars before Turn 6" (Grand Hotel hairpin) (2026 item 25.2). **[C]**
- **Grid.**
  - "Car number light panels / Grid Panel Placement: On the right-hand side of the grid." **[C]**
  - 2026 item 27 defines "the grid" as the section from the front of grid box #1 to the rear of grid box #30. **[C]** (the wording says #30 even though there are 22 cars).
  - "Cars may be removed from the grid through the pit lane exit." **[C]**
  - 2026: "Movement of the entry gate at the front of grid (grid position 5)". **[C]**
- **ERS hazard.** Mechanics go "to the pit exit area in front of the race control building" (2026). In 2025 the wording was "in front of race control building at pit entry". **[C]** The two years give conflicting locations.

### B.2 Grid clearing (when the grid is cleared)
- 2025 Art. 44.3: "When the ten (10) minute signal is shown, everybody except drivers, officials and team technical staff must leave the grid."
- 44.4: at the 5-minute signal, wheels must be fitted, tyre blankets disconnected, and "team personnel and equipment trolleys must commence leaving the grid."
- 44.5: at the 3-minute signal, no more than 16 team personnel per Competitor may be on the grid.
- 44.6: "When the one (1) minute signal is shown, engines should be started and all team personnel must leave the grid … by the time the fifteen (15) second signal is given taking all equipment with them."
- 2026 B5.5.2–B5.5.5 has identical content. For example, B5.5.5a: "Engines should be started and all team personnel must leave the grid, and the pit lane Fast Lane if working in accordance with B 5.3.3, by the time the fifteen (15) second signal is given taking all equipment with them."

### B.3 Formation lap
- 2026 B5.6.1: "When the green lights on the start gantry are illuminated, all F1 Cars on the grid able to do so should leave the grid and begin the formation lap with the pole position driver leading."
- B5.6.2: "When leaving the grid all drivers must respect the Pit Lane speed limit until they pass pole position."
- B5.6.3: "During the formation lap practice starts are forbidden and the formation must be kept as tight as possible."
- 2025 Art. 44.7–44.8 has the same text.

### B.4 Start light sequence
- **2025 Art. 44.10:** "When the cars starting from the grid return to the grid at the end of the formation lap or laps, they must stop within their respective starting grid positions, keeping their engines running. a) There will be a standing start, the signal being given by means of lights activated by the permanent starter. b) Once all the cars have come to a halt the five (5) second light will appear followed by the four, three, two and one second lights. At any time after the one-second light appears, the race will be started by extinguishing all red lights."
- **2026 B5.7.2 (Iss 06):** "a. There will be a standing start, the signal to start the TTCS being given by means of five red lights on the start gantry, operated by the permanent starter. b. Once all the F1 Cars have come to a halt the first red light will be illuminated followed sequentially by the second, third, fourth and fifth red lights. At any time after the fifth red light is illuminated, the signal to start the TTCS will be given by extinguishing all red lights on the start gantry. i. The time interval between the illumination of each of the five red lights in the sequence described above shall be one (1) second. ii. The time interval between the illumination of the fifth light and all lights being extinguished, to signal the start of the TTCS, is at the sole discretion of the permanent starter."
- **Pre-start blue phase.**
  - Monaco 2026 notes item 7.1: "once all F1 Cars starting from the grid have returned to the grid … the starting grid light panels will be illuminated blue (flashing) for 5 seconds and the information panel on the start gantry will display the message 'Pre-Start', following which the light sequence defined in to Article B5.7.2 … will commence."
  - This was later written into **Iss 09 B5.7.2b**: "Once all the F1 Cars have come to a halt, the Grid Light Panels will be illuminated blue (flashing) for five (5) seconds and the message 'Pre-Start' will be displayed on the start gantry, after which the first red light will be illuminated…"
- Item 7.2 of the same notes: the yellow starting-grid panel is "for information propose only".
- **False start.** 2025 Art. 48.1a: moving "after the four (4) second light is illuminated and before the start signal". 2026 B5.11.
  - At Monaco 2026, Pérez received a drive-through for starting from P16 instead of P18 (doc 69). He also received 10 s for "front right wheel … outside the starting box" at the restart after the red flag (doc 82). **[C]**

### B.5 Aborted start, delayed start, extra formation lap
- **2026 B5.8.1:** "If at any time after the formation lap has commenced and prior to the start of the TTCS a problem arises that could endanger the start, the Race Director may decide the start should be aborted…
  - a) Once all cars able to do so have completed the formation lap and returned to their allocated position on the grid, the orange lights on the start gantry will be illuminated, a board saying 'ABORTED START' will be displayed, and the message 'ABORTED START' will be sent to all Competitors.
  - b) … Team personnel and equipment trolleys will be permitted access to the grid…
  - c) … The starting procedure will begin again at the ten (10) minute signal…"
  - B5.8.3: "For each Aborted Start procedure completed the TTCS will be shortened by one (1) lap."
  - 2025 equivalent: Art. 47.1.
- **2026 B5.9.1a (Extra Formation Lap):** "…the orange lights on the start gantry will be illuminated, a board saying 'EXTRA FORMATION LAP' will be displayed and the message 'EXTRA FORMATION LAP' will be sent to all Competitors. After two (2) seconds the green lights on the start gantry will be illuminated, signalling that all cars able to do so must leave the grid and complete an extra formation lap."
  - B5.9.5: shortened by one lap each time.
  - 2025 equivalent: Art. 45.1 (same text; 45.1c: "Every time this happens the sprint session or the race will be shortened by one (1) lap.").
- **Delayed start.** 2026 B5.4.1 / 2025 Art. 46.1: before the formation lap starts, the orange lights on the start gantry are lit and a "DELAYED START" board is shown. The procedure restarts at the 10-minute signal.

### B.6 Standing restart after a red flag
- **2026 B5.15.2.** Signals are given at 10, 5, 3 and 1 min and 15 s.
  - At the 1-minute signal "the Safety Car will leave the Pit Lane and proceed to its resumption position on track". By default this is "at the end of the first timing sector". At Monaco it was before Turn 6, per notes item 25.2.
  - "At the resumption time the Pit Lane exit will be opened, all cars should leave the Pit Lane and proceed around the track to meet the Safety Car…"
- **B5.15.4 Standing Start Resumption:** "…the message 'STANDING START' will be sent to all Competitors, all FIA light panels will display 'SS', the Pit Lane exit will be closed, and the orange lights on the Safety Car will be extinguished. This will be the signal … that the Safety Car will be entering the Pit Lane at the end of that lap…
  - All other cars must go to the grid, take up their grid positions as defined by the Resumption Order, and follow the procedures set out in Article B5.7. The grid position of any F1 Cars allowed to take the Standing Start Resumption from the grid that are unable to reach the grid will remain vacant."
- **2025 Art. 58.** Under 58.6a the SC may remain in the pit lane, and then "all cars should leave the pit lane and proceed directly to the grid". 58.11 covers the standing start procedure ("all FIA light panels will display 'SS'"…, cars "return to the grid, take up their grid positions and follow the procedures set out in … Articles 44.10, 44.12 and 44.13").

### B.7 Where the start gantry is
- The regulations only say "start gantry" (lights on it: red ×5, green, orange; plus an information panel and boards). **[C]**
- Monaco: the FIA circuit maps (2025 V3; 2026 V2) show the **Start Line** and **Control Line** together on the start/finish straight, between T19 (Rascasse exit / Anthony Noghès) and T1, next to FIA light panel 1 and marshal post M21B. The gantry icon is drawn at that line. **[C]** from the map legend and symbols.
  - **[I]**: the start gantry spans the start/finish straight (Boulevard Albert 1er) at that line. Pole is on the RHS ("Pole RHS", 2025 pit drawing), and the grid light panels are on the RHS of the grid.

## C. Braking distance (marker) boards

### C.1 Governing regulation
- **FIA ISC Appendix O** (2026 edition, published 23/12/2025: https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/appendix_o_2026_published_23122025.pdf) is the circuit-design and homologation appendix.
  - A full-text search found **no clause that specifies braking/distance marker boards** or their distances. **[C]** as a negative finding, limited to this text search ("board", "marker", "distance", "brak*", "sign").
  - Closest provision, Art. 10 "Trackside advertising and structures": "All other advertising between the track and the first protection barrier is prohibited, with the exception of advertising panels added for a competition which must be approved… All such signs must be designed to break if impacted by a car into lightweight, harmless pieces… They must not be positioned on the exterior, or on either side of the exit, of corners and must not be situated less than 3 m from the track edge. Metallic materials are prohibited…" **[C]** Whether this applies to sponsor-branded distance boards is **[I]**.
  - Appendix O also asks circuit drawings to show "the distance measured in 50m increments from the start line along the track centreline". These are distance markings for the dossier, not braking boards. **[C]**
- No FIA document found sets standard values. Board values in FIA race-director notes appear only as location references. **[C]**

## Summary

- **Monaco pit lane.**
  - Entry is between T17 Rascasse and T18. SC1 line and pit-entry status panels are at the entry.
  - The lane runs on the inside of the start/finish straight, and the **garage block sits between the straight and the pit lane**.
  - The lane ends near pole position (RHS). A separate pit-exit road runs alongside the straight to T1 and rejoins **after T1 (Sainte Dévote)**. SC2 is after T1.
  - The speed limit was **60 km/h** in 2025 and 2026, by RD notes amending Art. 34.7 / B1.6.3a.
  - The pit exit light is on the LHS at the lane end. Red-flag queue: fast lane, first car near the last garage.
  - **Garage order 2025** (entry→exit): FIA/FOM, FIA, McLaren, Ferrari, Red Bull, Mercedes, Aston, Alpine, Haas, Racing Bulls, Williams, Sauber, FOM.
  - **Garage order 2026**: FIA, FOM, FIA, McLaren, Mercedes, Red Bull, Ferrari, Williams, Racing Bulls, Aston, (empty), Haas, Audi, Alpine, Cadillac.
  - Garages are about half normal width and multi-storey. The pit-wall gantry is on the first floor above the garages; there is no conventional pit wall. The top floor is for hospitality.
  - No FIA drawing gives F1 lane widths or total length. The only dimensions are the F2 sheet: 2.7 / 5.7 / 6.8 m from the fast-lane edge. The lane was lengthened in 2026 for Cadillac.
- **Start procedure.**
  - 2025: Art. 44 (grid clearing at 10 min, 3 min, and 1 min to 15 s; formation lap on green; lights per 44.10), 45 extra formation lap, 46 delayed start, 47 aborted start, 48 false start, 58 resumption.
  - 2026: B5.5, B5.6, **B5.7.2** (five reds at 1 s intervals; release at the starter's discretion), B5.8 aborted start, B5.9 extra formation lap, B5.11 false start, B5.15.4 standing restart.
  - Monaco 2026 added a 5 s blue flashing "Pre-Start" phase, which was later written into Iss 09 B5.7.2b.
  - Grid rows are 16 m apart, staggered. The start and control lines coincide on the main straight.
- **Braking boards.**
  - No FIA rule found prescribes them. Appendix O only regulates trackside panels generally (frangible, non-metallic, at least 3 m from the edge, not on the outside of corners).
  - FIA notes document specific boards: Monaco pit-exit 100 m (RHS), Barcelona pit-exit 100 m (RHS), Austria pit-entry 50 m, Spa T1 50 m, Monza T1/T4/T8 100 m, Mexico T12 150 m, Las Vegas (100 m boards blue). Press sources add Singapore T1 150/100 m (RHS) and Sochi T13 50 m.
  - Values vary by corner. Full per-corner sequences are not documented in official sources.
